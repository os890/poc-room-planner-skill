# Clearances and typical sizes

Rules of thumb for placing furniture so the layout works in real life. Values are guidance, not law – if the user's room is tight, say which rule you bend and by how much.

## General circulation
| What | Comfortable | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Main walkway | 90–120 cm | 60 cm |
| Passage between furniture | 75 cm | 50 cm |
| In front of a door (swing area) | keep the full swing free | – |
| Furniture next to a door frame | ≥ 30 cm | 10 cm |
| In front of radiators / heating | keep free (no sofa, no tall furniture); ≥ 10–15 cm for curtains | – |
| Doors of cabinets / wardrobes | door width + 10 cm in front | – |

## Living room
| What | Typical |
|---|---|
| Sofa seat height / depth | 42–45 cm / 55–60 cm (total depth 90–100 cm) |
| Sofa to coffee table | 40–45 cm |
| Sofa back to wall | 5–15 cm (looks less "pushed against the wall" with ~15 cm) |
| TV viewing distance | ~1.5–2.5 × screen diagonal (55″ ≈ 2.1–3.5 m); screen centre roughly at seated eye height (~1.0–1.2 m) |
| TV viewing angle | face the main seat; > ~15° off-axis feels crooked – shift or swivel the TV |
| Lowboard / sideboard depth | 40–45 cm; wall-mounted 15–20 cm above floor for cleaning |
| Rug | front sofa legs on the rug; ≥ 20–30 cm of floor visible to the walls |

## Dining
| What | Typical |
|---|---|
| Table for 4 | 120–160 × 80–90 cm; Ø 100–110 cm round |
| Table for 6 | 180–200 × 90–100 cm |
| Width per seat | 60 cm |
| Table edge to wall / obstacle behind a chair | 75–90 cm (pull out + sit down); 100+ cm if people walk behind |
| Table / seat height | 74–76 cm / 45–47 cm |

## Bedroom
| What | Typical |
|---|---|
| Bed sizes | 90/140/160/180 × 200 cm; mattress top 50–55 cm |
| Beside the bed | 60–70 cm (min. 50 cm) on each used side |
| Foot of bed to wall/furniture | 70–90 cm |
| Wardrobe depth | 60 cm (hinged doors need ~60–90 cm in front, sliding doors ~60 cm) |
| Bedside table | 40–50 cm wide, top ≈ mattress height |

## Kitchen
| What | Typical |
|---|---|
| Base cabinet depth / worktop height | 60 cm / 90–95 cm |
| Wall cabinet depth / bottom edge | 30–35 cm / 50–60 cm above worktop |
| Aisle between counters | 100–120 cm (min. 90 cm) |
| Landing space next to hob / sink / fridge | ≥ 40–60 cm |
| Work triangle (sink–hob–fridge) | total 4–8 m, no leg < 1.2 m or > 2.7 m |
| Dishwasher / oven door | needs ~60 cm free in front when open |

## Home office
| What | Typical |
|---|---|
| Desk | 120–160 × 70–80 cm, height 72–75 cm (or sit/stand) |
| Behind the desk edge (chair) | 90–100 cm |
| Screen distance | 50–80 cm, top edge at eye height |
| Window | light from the side, not behind the screen |

## Bathroom
| What | Typical |
|---|---|
| In front of WC / basin / shower | 70 cm (min. 55 cm) |
| Beside WC | 20–25 cm to wall |
| Basin height | 85–90 cm |
| Mirror cabinet bottom edge | ~120 cm |

## Heights for wall-mounted things
| What | Typical |
|---|---|
| Switches / sockets above furniture | sockets 20–30 cm above a sideboard/desk top, switches ~105 cm |
| Wall cabinets in living spaces | top line ≥ 20 cm below ceiling if indirect light is planned |
| Shelves over a sofa | ≥ 40 cm above the backrest (head room) |
| Wall lamp / reading light | 140–170 cm; above a sofa ~120–140 cm above seat |
| Pendant over a dining table | 60–70 cm above the table top |

## Light and power
- Layer the light: ambient (ceiling or indirect), task (reading/desk/kitchen counter), accent (shelves, small lamps). Make each layer switchable in the scene.
- Every free-standing powered item (TV, lamps, fountains, desk) needs a socket nearby – model it and the cable if the user cares.
- Existing outlets (ceiling, wall) are expensive to move: ask and keep fixtures there.
